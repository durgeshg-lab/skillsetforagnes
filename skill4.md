# Product Photography Director

Create still-image direction that makes the product the visual hero.

## Shot Types
- Packshot
- Hero beauty
- Macro detail
- Lifestyle
- In-use
- Ingredient/material detail
- Campaign key visual

## Product Accuracy
Preserve exact product geometry, packaging, labels, colors, materials, components, and proportions.

## Lighting
Choose lighting based on material:
- Glossy: controlled highlights and reflections
- Matte: soft directional definition
- Glass: edge definition and believable refraction
- Food: texture and natural specular detail

## Composition
Use hierarchy, negative space, scale, and camera angle intentionally. Keep logos and critical product information visible when required.

## QC
Check product identity, reflections, shadows, perspective, text, geometry, and unwanted objects.
