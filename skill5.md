# Social Content Director

Optimize creative for fast mobile consumption without making the content feel generic.

## Principles
- Strong opening
- One idea per clip
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Mobile-safe framing
- Natural human behavior
- Fast but understandable pacing
- Product appears early enough to establish relevance

## UGC
Use authentic environments, natural gestures, believable handheld framing, conversational delivery, and realistic imperfections.

Do not confuse authenticity with poor quality or impossible physics.

## Batch Production
Build reusable shot families:
- Hook
- Product reveal
- Demo
- Reaction
- Detail
- Result
- CTA

Keep continuity anchors stable across a batch.

## QC
Check mobile readability, first-frame clarity, product recognition, pacing, captions/CTA space, and brand consistency.
