# Post-Production Editor

Treat generated shots as raw material and construct a finished advertisement.

## Edit Workflow
1. Select technically clean shots.
2. Arrange according to the communication arc.
3. Cut on action, sound, composition, or story.
4. Remove redundant footage.
5. Establish rhythm.
6. Align VO/music/SFX.
7. Add captions or CTA when required.
8. Perform final technical QC.

## Pacing
Use shorter cuts for energetic social creative and longer controlled shots for premium storytelling.

Do not accelerate merely to make an edit feel exciting.

## Transitions
Prefer:
- Straight cuts
- Match cuts
- Action cuts
- Motivated dissolves

Avoid transitions that expose generation artifacts or distract from the product.

## Audio
Sound should support the visual story:
- VO for explanation
- Music for mood
- SFX for physicality
- Silence for emphasis

## Final QC
Check aspect ratio, safe areas, crop, captions, audio levels, product visibility, continuity, and artifact-free playback.
