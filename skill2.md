# Performance Ad Creative

Design ads around a measurable communication objective while preserving brand truth.

## Structure
Hook → problem/desire → product → proof/demo → benefit → CTA.

## Hook Design
Generate distinct hook families:
- Problem
- Curiosity
- Demonstration
- Before/after
- Social proof
- Product surprise
- Direct benefit

Do not use misleading claims.

## Variants
Vary one major creative dimension at a time:
- Hook
- Opening visual
- Product demonstration
- Talent delivery
- CTA

Keep product and brand identity stable so tests are meaningful.

## CTA
Make the desired action obvious and appropriate to the supplied offer.

## QC
Reject clickbait that contradicts the product, unsupported claims, confusing openings, weak product visibility, or CTAs that appear too late to matter.
